Privacy Policy · سياسة الخصوصية — October 2026

Summary

Card Game Calculator keeps score for Trix Complex and Estimation. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no internet permission.

What the app stores

  • The player names you type, the scores you enter, your rule settings and your language choice.
  • All of it is saved only on your device (app storage) and never leaves it.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes everything. Any single game can be deleted inside the app.

Permissions & third parties

The app requests no special permissions and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking. "Copy results" only places text on your clipboard when you tap it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For questions, contact the developer through the email shown on the app's Google Play page.
