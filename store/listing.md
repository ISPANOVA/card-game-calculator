## English
Short description (≤80):
Score keeper for Trix Complex & Estimation — no ads, offline, Arabic & English.

Full description:
Card Game Calculator keeps score for your real card nights, so nobody argues over the paper again.

TRIX COMPLEX
• 4 kingdoms, each with Trix and Complex, tracked for you
• Complex: King of hearts −75, Queens −25, Diamonds −10, tricks −15
• Doubling: the taker pays double, the doubler collects
• Trix: just tap the players in the order they finished (+200 / 150 / 100 / 50)
• Solo or partners (1 & 3 vs 2 & 4) with team totals

ESTIMATION
• 18 rounds (13 normal + 5 fast) with over / under shown live
• Call, With, Dash, Dash call, Risk, only winner / only loser
• Sa'aydeh: when everybody misses, the next round counts double
• Save the bids before playing, enter the tricks after
• Every value is adjustable to your table's rules

ALSO
• Live score preview before you save, and checks that every hand adds up
• Edit or undo any round
• Game history saved automatically on your phone
• Arabic and English, beautiful card-table design
• No ads, no account, no internet needed — your data never leaves your phone
